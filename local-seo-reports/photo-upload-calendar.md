# GBP photo strategy and upload calendar

Date: 2026-11-25. **Current photo library not audited** (no GBP access). The website already has about 30 genuine project images in `dist/assets/img/` (for example `backyard-bed-cleanup.jpg`, `mulched-beds-og.jpg`, `shaded-front-yard-og.jpg`). Those can be uploaded to the GBP now, provided they're Yardex's own work.

## Rules
- **Only real Yardex photos.** No stock, no AI images, no competitors' work.
- Get the client's permission. Don't show house numbers, licence plates or people's faces without consent.
- Horizontal or square, at least 720 px, well lit, no text overlays or watermarks.
- What makes a photo count: it shows real completed work. Filenames, EXIF geotags and GPS data **do not** improve Maps rankings, so don't spend time on them. Descriptive filenames and alt text still help **on the website** for accessibility and image search.
- Mix it up: completed work, before/after pairs, crew and equipment, logo and cover.

## Peak-season cadence
| Period | Uploads/week | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Dec–Feb | 1 every 2 weeks | Best-of-2026 shots, team photo, equipment, logo and cover refresh |
| Mar–May | 2–3 | Spring cleanup before/after, first cuts, dethatching*, aeration cores, overseeding, mulch, sod* |
| Jun–Aug | 2 | Striping, edging close-ups, hedge trimming, garden weeding, vacation care |
| Sep–Nov | 2–3 | Fall aeration and overseeding, leaf removal before/after, fall cleanups, final cut |

\* Only if the service is confirmed.

## Weekly shot list (in season)
1. One **completed lawn** (wide shot, stripes visible).
2. One **before/after pair** from the week's best job, shot from the same angle and distance.
3. Every second week: one **crew or equipment** shot (crew in uniform, mower and trailer).
4. Each month: one **service-specific** shot for the month's focus (cleanup, aeration, mulch, hedge).
