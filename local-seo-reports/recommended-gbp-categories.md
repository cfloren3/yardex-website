## Change rules

1. **Record the current categories** (a screenshot) and the last 3 months of GBP Performance before editing.
2. **If the primary is already "Lawn care service": leave it.** Add or adjust secondaries only.
3. **If the primary is something else** (for example Landscaper): changing it is the highest-impact and highest-risk single edit. Expect lawn-care rankings to improve and landscaping rankings to soften. Edits to the primary category can also trigger re-verification. Make the change **in December–January** (lowest demand), change nothing else in the same 4 weeks, and run Local Falcon before and 2 and 4 weeks after.
4. Change **one thing at a time** during the measurement window, so any movement can be attributed.
