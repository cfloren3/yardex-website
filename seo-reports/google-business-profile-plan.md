## 5. Review acquisition workflow (genuine reviews only)

1. **Trigger:** after a first-month milestone for weekly clients, and the day after any one-time job (cleanup, sod, dethatch).
2. **Ask:** a personal text from Carter with the GBP short review link (GBP → "Ask for reviews"). No incentives, no gating (don't only ask happy customers), no review stations.
3. **Prompt for specifics:** "If you're happy to, mention the service and your neighbourhood — it helps other Guelph homeowners." This naturally produces service and location words in reviews without scripting them.
4. **Track:** monthly count and velocity (Local Falcon or a spreadsheet). Goal: steady 4–8 new reviews/month in season. Velocity matters more than a burst.

## 6. Review response templates (personalize every time)

- **5★, specific:** "Thanks, [first name]! Glad the [service] in [area] turned out the way you wanted. See you at the next visit. – Carter"
- **5★, no text:** "Thank you, [first name] — we appreciate you choosing Yardex. – Carter"
- **Mixed/negative:** "Hi [first name], thanks for telling us. I'm sorry [issue]. I'd like to make it right — I'll text you today at the number on file. – Carter" (then take it offline; never argue publicly or reveal details).

## 7. Photo strategy

- Upload 3–5 real photos per week in season: before/after pairs, crew at work (with consent), equipment, finished stripes and edges.
- Descriptive file names before upload (`spring-cleanup-guelph-before.jpg`); geotags are not a ranking factor, so skip them.
- No customer house numbers or faces without permission.
