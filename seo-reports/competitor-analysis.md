# Yardex Guelph — Competitor Analysis

**Date:** 2026-11-25

## Method and limits (read first)

- Competitor websites **could not be fetched** from this environment. All ten attempted domains failed DNS through the sandbox network policy, so titles, H1s, page counts and site speed could not be inspected directly.
- Evidence comes from web-search result pages and snippets (a US-based index, **not** google.ca and not localized to Guelph), plus third-party directories (ThreeBestRated, HomeStars, BBB, Bark, Kijiji).
- **No organic positions, Maps positions, review counts from Google, backlink counts or traffic estimates are claimed.** They need Local Falcon (grid scans), GSC and Semrush/Ahrefs — see the roadmap.
- Several named competitors from the brief returned **no matching business** in search: "Leaf It Alone", "A Cut Above Yard Services" (Guelph), "CH Lawn Care", "Gelderman". They may exist under different names or have little web presence. Confirm the exact business names from the Google Maps pack before the first Local Falcon scan.

## Exploitable gaps (without copying anyone)

1. **Service-specific pages for restoration work** — dethatching, sodding and hedge trimming are listed by competitors only as bullets or on directories. Dedicated, genuinely useful pages are the gap the new pages fill.
2. **Puslinch** — directories own these results. A dedicated, honest page (septic beds, wells, County waste collection, route-based availability) is distinctive.
3. **Pricing honesty** — publish pricing factors on every service page (done) and real "from" prices once confirmed.
4. **Original evidence** — before/after photo sets and short case studies per service (template in `content-calendar.md`). The thing competitors can't copy.
5. **Online booking or instant quote** — PropertyWerks's advantage. Consider a simple "book a first cut" date picker later (conversion, not SEO).
6. **ThreeBestRated link** points to yardexguelph.com, not .ca — fix for consistency (see backlink plan).
